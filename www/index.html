FOOD, WATER, SLEEP

Fuel the routine, not a diet.

  • WATER150–250 ml on waking. 100–150 ml every 30–45 minutes through the day. About 6–8 cups total, adjusted for heat, sweat, and thirst. Pale straw urine is a useful guide.
  • BREAKFASTWithin 1–2 hours of waking. Include a protein source and a normal carbohydrate portion.
  • LUNCHHalf vegetables or salad, one quarter protein, one quarter rice, roti, or other carbohydrate.
  • DINNERSimilar balance, slightly smaller if that suits you. No need to skip meals.
  • SLEEPTarget 7–8 hours. Consistent bed and wake times matter more than a perfect night.

General guidance only. Follow your doctor or dietitian for medical conditions, pregnancy, or prescribed diets. No fat-loss spot reduction is promised.
